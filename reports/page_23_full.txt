Module 9: NLP & Qualitative Analysis Module
Parses and enriches qualitative text fields: about_company, pros, cons, analysis strings. Auto-generates pros/cons for companies with missing records
using KPI-threshold rules. Extracts CAGR numbers from analysis.xlsx text fields.
Sprint: Sprint 5 (Days 29–35)

Auto pros/cons generator
Rule engine: 12 pro rules (ROE>20%, FCF positive 5yr, D/E=0, revenue CAGR>15%, etc.) + 12 con rules (D/E>2, FCF negative 3yr, OPM declining, etc.). Confidence score 0–100%.

Module 10: Statistical Analysis & Clustering Module
Uses scikit-learn for KMeans clustering of companies by financial profile. Computes portfolio-level statistics, correlation matrices, and outlier detection.
Provides insight into hidden company groupings.
Sprint: Sprint 5–6 (Days 29–42)
