10. Sector Allocation: 'Banking' holds the largest cumulative weight across equity fund portfolios, reflecting the benchmark-heavy composition of Indian equity funds. [Chart 13]

11. Risk-Adjusted Leader: 'ICICI Pru Liquid Fund - Regular - Growth' achieves the highest Sharpe ratio of 7.68, offering the best return per unit of risk among all 40 schemes. [Chart 18]

12. Rolling Volatility Spikes: Top-5 equity funds by AUM show pronounced volatility spikes during market stress events, with annualised 30-day vol regularly exceeding 20% in correction phases. [Chart 19]

13. Alpha vs Drawdown: Higher-risk-graded funds tend to cluster in the high-alpha / high-drawdown quadrant, while Low-risk funds exhibit stable but modest alpha – highlighting the classic risk-reward trade-off. [Chart 20]

14. Dominant Payment Mode: 'Net Banking' is the most-used payment channel across all transaction types, reflecting the shift to digital payment infrastructure in retail investing. [Chart 21]

15. Monthly Investment Trends: SIP volumes show a sustained upward trend over time, while Lumpsum and Redemption flows exhibit greater month-to-month variability. [Chart 22]

16. Income-Driven Investment Scale: Investment amounts rise progressively with income bands – investors in the >100L income group invest significantly larger lumpsum amounts, while lower-income bands drive high-volume, low-ticket SIPs. [Chart 23]

17. Concentration in Top Holdings: 'Axis Bank Ltd' leads with Rs.16326 Cr aggregate market value across all fund portfolios, highlighting high concentration risk in index-heavy stocks. [Chart 24]

18. Folio Category Mix: Equity folios dominate and grew from 9.28 Cr to 18.28 Cr, while debt and hybrid folios remained relatively stable – confirming the equity-first preference of new retail investors. [Chart 25]
