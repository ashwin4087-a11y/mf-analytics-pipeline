Module 2: Financial Ratio Engine
Computes 50+ KPIs for every company-year combination across all 14 available years. Handles all edge cases: division by zero, negative equity,
debt-free companies, bank/NBFC D/E carve-out, and CAGR sign anomalies.
Sprint: Sprint 2 (Days 8–14)
