Mission
Build a self-contained, production-grade financial intelligence system enabling analysts to query, screen, score, and compare all 92 Nifty 100 companies using structured fundamental data — without relying on third-party paid platforms.
Vision
Become the definitive internal reference for Nifty 100 fundamental analysis — reproducible, auditable, and version-controlled from public financial filings.
Principle
Every insight must be traceable to a formula, every formula to a source column, every column to a dataset file. No black-box numbers.
